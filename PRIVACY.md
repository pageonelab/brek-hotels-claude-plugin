# Brek Hotel Search plugin privacy notice

Publisher: PageOne Lab. Contact: leo@pageonelab.com.

This notice describes the Brek Hotel Search plugin's hotel-research instructions and connector configuration. The plugin contains no executable scripts, database, background collection, or payment tools.

For a requested hotel search, Claude may send Brek the destination or hotel name/identifier, stay dates, room and guest counts, child ages where supplied, currency, and requested search filters. The instructions limit requests to those trip criteria and do not request unrelated conversation contents, credentials, payment details, or traveler identities.

Requests go to the declared Brek connector at https://mcp.brek.com/mcp, or to https://www.brek.com through Claude's webpage tools. If the traveler requests independent source comparisons, Claude may also search or read the requested public booking sources. Query links can contain stay criteria. Network requests can produce ordinary technical logs, such as IP addresses and request times, at the services involved.

The instruction package does not itself retain user data. Claude's conversation storage and Brek's service logs, price caches, inventory providers, and website processing have their own retention settings and policies; this plugin makes no fixed retention-period promise for those services. For Brek's website policy and data requests, see https://www.brek.com/privacy or contact the publisher above. Claude's own privacy policy governs its processing.

Hotel research does not create a reservation or take payment. If the traveler follows a link to sign in or book, the website's separate terms and privacy policy apply. This plugin is intended for adult travelers and is not directed at children.
